The story

Born on 11 June 1993 in the village of Moosa, Mansa district, Punjab, Shubhdeep Singh Sidhu grew up immersed in Punjabi folk traditions before earning a degree in Electrical Engineering. Music pulled him in a different direction — he began writing lyrics for other artists in 2016, penning "License" for singer Ninja, before releasing his own debut single "G-Wagon" in 2017.

His 2018 debut album PBX 1, released under T-Series, charted internationally and marked the start of a run of hits that carried Punjabi music to new global audiences, with collaborations spanning Indian and international artists. His influence is widely credited by a new generation of Punjabi and South Asian musicians who followed.

On 29 May 2022, Sidhu Moose Wala was shot and killed in Jawaharke village, Mansa district, at the age of 28. His music and legacy continue to be celebrated by fans around the world.
